# Hook library

The first 5-15 seconds decide whether someone stays. A hook makes a **specific promise** and
**opens a question** the viewer can't answer yet. Pick the formula that suits the topic, and
don't use the same one twice in a row on the channel (check `content-log.md`).

The examples use a history niche. Swap in your own.

## Long-form hooks (first 15-30 seconds)

1. **Cold open in the middle of the action.** Start at the most dramatic moment, then rewind.
   "At 4:07 in the morning, the river turned black. By sunrise, nobody in the town was alive. To understand why, we have to go back forty years."
2. **The contradiction.** Two facts that shouldn't both be true.
   "Rome had aqueducts, concrete and a standing army of 400,000. It still fell to people it called barbarians."
3. **The question nobody asks.** "Everyone knows the Titanic hit an iceberg. Almost nobody asks why it was going so fast."
4. **The bold claim.** Stated as fact, then backed up by the video. "The worst decision in British history took eleven minutes."
5. **The stakes.** What was won or lost, in numbers. "One wrong signal cost 20,000 men their lives in an afternoon."
6. **The object.** Start from one physical thing. "This is a 2,000-year-old receipt. It tells us more about the fall of an empire than any history book."
7. **The countdown promise** (for ranked lists). "Number 7 is strange. Number 1 is still classified."
8. **The "you're wrong about this".** "You've probably been told the Dark Ages were dark. They weren't, and the reason the myth exists is the interesting part."
9. **The timeline squeeze.** "In 1910 it was the richest city on Earth. By 1930 it was underwater."
10. **The person.** One human at the centre. "Nobody remembers Stanislav Petrov. You're alive because of what he didn't do."
11. **The open mystery.** "The ship was found drifting with the table set for dinner. The crew was never found."
12. **The before-and-after.** Show the end state first. "This empty field used to be the largest market in Europe."

After any long-form hook, use a **promise line** within 30 seconds: what this video will cover,
and why it's worth the full runtime. "By the end, you'll know exactly why it happened, and why it could happen again."

## Shorts hooks (first 1-2 seconds, the first line is the hook)

13. **Start mid-sentence.** "...and that's why the pyramids were never built by slaves."
14. **The direct challenge.** "You can't name the shortest war in history. It lasted 38 minutes."
15. **The number.** "This city lost 90% of its people in a single week."
16. **The "wait, what?" fact.** "Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing than to the pyramids being built."
17. **The visual pointer** (only if the clip clearly shows it). "Look at the man on the left. He's about to change history."
18. **The ranked teaser.** "The third one is the reason you have a weekend."
19. **The myth flip.** "Vikings didn't wear horned helmets. Here's who made that up."
20. **The unanswered question.** "Why is there a perfectly preserved ship in the middle of a desert?"

## Rules

- Be specific. "A city disappeared" is weak. "A city of 40,000 disappeared in one night" works.
- No throat-clearing. Never open with "Hey guys", "In this video", "Welcome back" or "Before we start".
- **Never promise something the video doesn't deliver.** Viewers who feel tricked leave early,
  and early drop-off hurts the video in recommendations more than a weaker hook would.
- Don't ask for subscribes or likes in the hook.

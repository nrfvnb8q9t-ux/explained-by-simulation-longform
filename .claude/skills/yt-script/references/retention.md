# Keeping people watching (long-form)

Long videos earn watch hours and mid-video ads, but only if people keep watching. Build the
script around these beats.

## Structure for a 15-30 minute video

| Beat | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00-0:15 | One formula from `hooks.md`. |
| Promise | by 0:30 | What they'll get by the end and why it's worth the time. |
| Context | 0:30-2:00 | Only the background they need. Get to the story fast. |
| Act 1 | ~2-8 min | The setup. End it on an open question. |
| Re-hook | every 2-4 min | A line that restarts curiosity (see below). |
| Act 2 | middle | Escalation: complications, turning points, the strongest material. |
| Low point / twist | ~70% in | The surprise, reversal or darkest moment. |
| Payoff | final 15% | Answer every question you opened. |
| Bridge out | final 30 sec | Point to one specific next video on the channel. No generic "thanks for watching". |

For ranked lists, count down and put the strongest item last. Tease it in the hook and once in the middle.

## Re-hooks (use one every 2-4 minutes)

- **Open loop:** "But that wasn't the strangest part. We'll get there."
- **Stakes reset:** "Up to this point, it was a disaster. What happened next made it a catastrophe."
- **Direct question:** "So why didn't anyone stop it?"
- **Time jump:** "Three weeks later, a letter arrived."
- **Contrast:** "On paper, they should have won easily."

## Writing rules

- **Write for the ear.** Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Read it aloud in your head.
  The text-to-speech voice will say exactly what's written, so spell out numbers and abbreviations
  the way they should be spoken ("nineteen fourteen", "the U.N.").
- **Concrete over abstract.** Names, dates, places, numbers, physical details.
- **Change something every 20-40 seconds:** a new visual, new location, new person, a number on screen.
  This gives the editor a cut point and stops it feeling like a slideshow.
- **No filler.** Cut "it's important to note", "interestingly", "in conclusion", "let's dive in".
- **One mid-video ask, at most.** If you ask people to subscribe, do it once, after a strong payoff around
  40-60% through, in one sentence.
- **Mid-video ads:** videos over 8 minutes can have them. Leave natural pauses at the end of acts. Don't
  end a sentence on a cliffhanger right before a pause, because the ad will cut in.
